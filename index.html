    Items

    Main items needed to make these cookies

    • Sugar 3/4 to 1 cup of granulated sugar.
    • Flour 2 1/4 cups of all-purpose flour to build structure.
    • Eggs 2 large eggs, beaten.
    • Butter 1 cup (220g/2 sticks) of unsalted butter, softened.
    • Vanilla Extract 1 to 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract.
    • Baking Soda 1 teaspoon of baking soda and sometimes baking powder to help the cookies rise.
    • Salt 1/2 teaspoon of salt to balance the sweetness and enhance flavor.
    • Chocolate Chips 2 cups of semi-sweet chocolate chips.

      Frequently asked questions

      How many cookies does each recipe make?

      Each recipe starts with a 12-cookie yield. Choose a different batch size to scale the ingredient amounts.

      Can I make a different number of cookies?

      Yes. Enter the number you want in the recipe picker, then select Show ingredients to see the adjusted amounts.

      How can I get thicker, chewier cookies?

      Chill the dough for about 30 minutes before baking. This can help limit spreading.

      How should I store baked cookies?

      Let them cool completely, then store them in an airtight container to help keep them fresh.

      Are these recipes safe for people with food allergies?

      They are not guaranteed allergen-free. Check every ingredient label and take steps to prevent cross-contact.
